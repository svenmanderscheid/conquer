# Aligned village buildings

Built-in Imagegen edit mode; original assets retained. Runtime WebP copies limited to 512 px. Academy is the camera/orientation reference.

## hospital

Saved asset: hospital_aligned.png; delivery: runtime/hospital_aligned.webp

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target image 1: hospital village sprite. Image 2: academy, the exact orientation and perspective reference. Redraw ONLY the target building, rotate its architectural footprint to match the academy: entrance facade faces diagonally toward LOWER RIGHT, left side wall visible on the LEFT. This is the opposite yaw from the current target. All roof ridges, stairs, side wings, fences and props must follow this coherent new orientation. Preserve the target's identity, colors, distinctive symbols, architecture and props; no academy elements. Keep soft chunky storybook shapes, matte broad cel shading, dark brown outlines, same high-angle game camera and illumination. Give it a slightly broader, substantial main volume while retaining the original recognizable design. Single isolated building sprite, centered fully visible with small transparent margins. Genuinely transparent alpha background; no terrain, text, UI, floor slab or cast shadow rectangle. Do not just rotate the flat image.
