# Research illustrations

Original code-native SVG artwork created for Conquer from the visual language of the supplied LOK screenshots. No research icon was cropped or extracted from a screenshot. Cyan armor identifies infantry, green hoods identify archers, and magenta horse armor identifies cavalry. Large foreground symbols identify HP, defense, attack, movement, storage and training effects. Economy resource portraits reuse the separately supplied existing ui-resources PNGs; the three resource-*.svg marks identify production, capacity and gathering.
